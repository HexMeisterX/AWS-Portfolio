<ins>Challenges:<ins>
- Manual Booking System: Orders and reservations get mixed up. leading to customer frustration and lost revenue
- No Centralized Data: Difficult to track customer preferences or manage confirmations across the team
- Scaling Costs: Hiring more staff to handle growing demand is expensive and unsustainable long-term

### **Solution: Migrate to AWS!**

<ins>AWS Benefits:<ins>
- Amazon S3: To host your new static website and stores all menu images securely in the cloud
- AWS Cognito: To manage customer sign-ins and provides secure access to your website
- Amazon RDS/DynamoDB: Centralizes all customer data, bookings, and orders in one reliable database

<ins>More AWS Benefits:<ins>
- Zero Mix-Ups: Digital orders eliminate manual errors entirely
- Available 24/7: Customer can book and order anytime
- Customer Insights: Track preferences and identify repeat customer to deliver personalized, memorable service every visit

<ins>Cost Efficiency:<ins>
- Pay-As-You-Go: No upfront infrastructure costs, your bill reflects your actual usage, nothing more
- Reduced Labor: Automation handles order processing, freeing your team to focus on exceptional in-person service
- Elastic Scalability: Peak seasons, promotions, or viral moments, the platform grows with demand instantly

<ins>Why AWS?:<ins>
- 99.99% Uptime Reliability: Your business stays online around the clock, ensuring customers can always reach you
- 256-bit Enterprise Security: Bank-grade encryption protects every customer record and payment details on your platform
- Infinite Growth & Ready to Scale: As Harbour Table expands, AWS scales automatically
